This is "The Third Coming", inspired by Alan Becker's series. It is an AI Desktop Mate that's somewhat mischievous but very customizable. Entirely vibecoded. Simply download the installer and run.

Features:
Controlling things on your screen, interacting with elements.
Completely local, uses ollama.
Has a chat room.
Generally low latency.
Can build blocks.
And more, just right click on the third coming!

Caution: The Third Coming may act similar to a virus. Simply turn off mischief and adventures to minimize any potential damage.
